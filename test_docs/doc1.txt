The Rung Strategy: A Real Trading Approach

The rung strategy is a systematic approach to position sizing and profit-taking
in volatile assets. Rather than buying or selling all at once, positions are
built and reduced in discrete steps ("rungs") at predetermined price levels.

Key benefits: reduces timing risk, enforces discipline, avoids emotional
decision-making during volatility. Key drawback: can underperform a simple
buy-and-hold approach in strongly trending markets, since capital is held back
for rungs that may never be reached.

This approach works best for assets with genuine, cyclical volatility rather
than assets in a persistent one-directional trend.
