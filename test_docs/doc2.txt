Risk Management in Volatile Markets

Effective risk management requires accepting that no single strategy works in
all market conditions. Diversification across asset classes, position sizing
based on conviction level, and maintaining cash reserves for opportunities are
all real, practical techniques.

A common mistake is over-concentration in a single thesis, even a well-reasoned
one. Markets can remain irrational longer than a trader can remain solvent.
Real discipline means having predefined exit criteria before entering a
position, not deciding under pressure during a drawdown.
